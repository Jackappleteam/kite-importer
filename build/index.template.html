Move to Kite

Turns some of your BotGhost and inventor.bot commands into Kite JSON that you paste into Kite's JSON import. Everything runs in your browser, and your commands are never uploaded anywhere.

Unofficial. This isn't made, endorsed or supported by Kite, BotGhost or inventor.bot. Both builders' terms restrict automated tools and scripts, so check their terms before using it. It only reads your own commands, only when you click it, and it can't move everything: messages and some actions are converted, and anything else is listed so you can rebuild it.

1. Add the bookmark

Drag this to your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B or Cmd+Shift+B shows the bar).

Move to Kite

2. Open your bot and click it

Open your bot's commands in the BotGhost dashboard, or a bot in the inventor.bot dashboard, wait until it loads, then click the bookmark. Press Open converter in the box. This page fills in by itself.

3. Copy each command into Kite

Press Copy Kite JSON next to a command, then paste it into the JSON import in your Kite app. Check anything marked "check after import".

Converter

If the page didn't fill in, press Copy in the bookmark's box (or Save as file) and paste or choose it here.

No bookmarks bar? Use the console

Open the dashboard, open the browser console (F12), paste this and press Enter. It is the same script as the bookmark.
